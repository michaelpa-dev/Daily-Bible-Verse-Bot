# Privacy Policy for Daily Bible Verse Bot

**Effective Date: 2026-04-01**

## Data We May Process
- **User IDs**: We may collect user IDs from Discord to identify users of the bot.
- **Server IDs**: Server IDs are collected to associate users with their respective servers.
- **Message Content**: We process message content only when necessary to respond to commands.
- **Interaction Metadata**: Metadata related to interactions with the bot may also be collected.

## Subscriptions/Preferences for Daily Verses
We may allow users to subscribe to daily verses and manage their preferences.

## Logs/Diagnostics
We may collect logs for diagnostic and troubleshooting purposes.

## Optional Analytics
Users may have the option to provide anonymized usage data for analytics purposes.

## Data Not Intentinally Collected
- **Passwords**: We do not collect user passwords.
- **Payment Information**: We do not collect payment information as the bot is free to use.

## How We Use Your Data
- To provide the service effectively.
- To troubleshoot issues.
- To prevent abuse of the bot.

## Data Sharing
- We share data with Discord as the platform that hosts the bot.
- Hosting providers may have access to some data but we do not sell any data.

## Data Retention
- We minimize data retention and delete data when a user unsubscribes or leaves.
- Logs are retained for a reasonable period necessary for diagnostics.

## User Rights
- Users can request deletion of their data by contacting us.

## Privacy of Children
We take children's privacy seriously and do not knowingly collect data from minors.

## Security
We implement security measures to protect user data.

## International Transfers
Your data may be processed in countries outside your own.

## Changes
We may update this policy; please check back for changes.

## Contact
For any inquiries, please contact us via GitHub repo issues or email placeholder.
